# Privacy Policy for HUSH

**Last updated:** January 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HUSH ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application HUSH (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We prioritize your privacy and aim to collect only the minimum amount of data necessary to provide our services.

*   **App Usage Data:** We collect non-personal, aggregated data about how the App is used (e.g., features accessed, session duration) to help us improve performance and efficienty.
*   **Notification Data:** Data related to your notifications (e.g., app names, arrival times) is processed **locally on your device** to enable the core focus and filtering functionality. We do not transmit the content of your notifications to external servers.
*   **Subscription Status:** We collect data regarding your subscription status (e.g., Free vs. Premium, expiration dates) through secure third-party providers (RevenueCat and Google Play) to manage your access to premium features.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

*   **No Personal Identification:** We do not collect your name, address, phone number, or physical location.
*   **No Private Content:** We do not collect, read, or store your contacts, messages, photos, or other personal media.
*   **No Data Selling:** We do not sell your data to third parties.

## 3. How Data Is Used

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

*   **Functionality:** To operate the App and deliver its core features, such as notification filtering and focus modes.
*   **Analytics:** To understand user behavior and improve the App's stability and user experience.
*   **Premium Management:** To verify and manage your premium subscription and restore purchases.

## 4. Data Storage & Security

*   **Local Processing:** The majority of your data, including sensitive notification details, is processed and stored locally on your device.
*   **Security Measures:** We use industry-standard security measures to protect the integrity of your data.
*   **Third-Party Transfer:** Subscription-related data is transmitted securely to our payment and entitlement providers (Google Play and RevenueCat) via encrypted connections.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to help us operate the App. These parties have their own privacy policies governing the use of your data.

*   **Google Play Services:** For app distribution, billing, and core Android services. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   **RevenueCat:** For managing subscriptions and in-app purchases. [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

## 6. User Rights

*   **Uninstall:** You may stop all collection of information by uninstalling the App.
*   **Subscription Management:** You can manage or cancel your subscriptions directly through the Google Play Store settings.
*   **Data Removal:** You have the right to request the deletion of any data we may hold (if any). Please contact us at the email below.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from our servers.
